# Attio CRM: Call Handling Rules

Use these rules to keep Attio current after every prospect interaction and to preserve the buying context needed for the next call.

## After every call

1. Add the Circleback call recording to a **Note** in Attio as soon as the call is over.
2. Record the prospect's stated annual return volume and their estimated first-year volume through Cranston. Do not substitute a guess when the prospect has not given one.
3. Capture the buyer's stated reasons for evaluating Cranston, the terms they use, stakeholders, objections, next steps, and any promised follow-up.

## Prospect value

Set **Prospect Value** to the prospect's stated annual return volume multiplied by **$40**.

`Prospect Value = annual returns processed × $40`

Example: a firm processing 2,000 returns per year has a prospect value of **$80,000**.

This represents the full potential value based on the volume they say they process annually; it is not necessarily their first-year contract value.

## First-year contract value

Set **First-Year Contract Value** to the number of returns the prospect says they are likely to process through Cranston in year one multiplied by **$40**.

`First-Year Contract Value = expected first-year Cranston returns × $40`

Example: a firm that processes 2,000 returns per year may expect to begin with 400 returns through Cranston. Its first-year contract value would be **$16,000**.

## Lead section: key drivers and terms for the next call

Maintain a dedicated section on every lead titled **Key drivers and terms for the next call**. Use it to record the prospect's own motivations and the exact language that resonated with them.

Include:

- The business or workflow pain they want Cranston to solve.
- Their urgency and the consequence of doing nothing.
- Words or phrases they used to describe the desired outcome.
- Any relevant proof points, product areas, or demo moments to revisit.
- Open questions, objections, and the specific next step they agreed to.

Example:

- The prospect cannot hire enough talent.
- They want to adopt the next wave of AI before competitors do.
- They are afraid of being outcompeted if they do not modernize their workflow.

Bring these drivers back into future calls in the prospect's language and connect them to the next demonstration. For example: “You mentioned wanting to stay ahead of firms that are adopting AI. Let’s show you the cutting-edge workflow we discussed and test whether it can create that advantage in your team’s actual process.”

Follow Max's pattern: listen for the prospect's exact motivation, repeat it back concretely, and make the next demo feel tailored to that motivation rather than generic.
