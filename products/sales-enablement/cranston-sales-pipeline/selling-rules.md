# Selling Rules

## Sell a simple, verifiable AI product

Sell an AI product with an output the customer can easily inspect and determine is correct. The value should be visible, concrete, and straightforward to validate.

## Focus on frequent, repeatable work

Prioritize work the customer performs repeatedly and often, where the correct process does not require new creative judgment each time. Repetition makes the workflow easier to adopt, trust, and scale.

## Show immediate labor-cost savings

The product should clearly save money now compared with hiring a person to perform the same work. Make the comparison concrete: show the recurring work, the human time or headcount it would otherwise require, and the cost avoided through the product.

## Prove traceability and correctness

Show where each important piece of data came from and how it connects to the output. The customer should be able to trace the data back to its source, verify that it is correct, and see that the result is grounded in the underlying documents and facts.

## Address data security in the customer's language

Explain that customer data is stored on US servers and meets SOC 2-level security requirements or the equivalent. Do not bring up SOC 2 compliance proactively; discuss it only if the prospect mentions SOC 2 by name.
