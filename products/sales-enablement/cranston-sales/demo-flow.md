# Cranston Demo Flow

Use this when showing Cranston to a prospect. The demo should tell the story of one return, one source trail, and one professional decision.

## Before The Demo

- Know who is attending and what each person owns: preparation, review, operations, technology, or purchase.
- Know the firm's return mix, tax engine, hosting setup, workpaper process, and stated bottleneck.
- Choose a return and workflow that match confirmed support.
- Decide the next proof step before the call starts.

## Demo Sequence

1. Restate the problem.
   Repeat the buyer's pain in workflow language before showing the product.

2. Introduce the package.
   Show the current files and prior-year context. Point out missing, duplicated, wrong-year, or questionable evidence.

3. Follow one number.
   Open a tax value, show the source document or ledger support behind it, and explain how a reviewer checks the trail.

4. Show an exception.
   Use one missing item, year-over-year anomaly, diagnostic, or uncertain treatment to show where Cranston stops and asks for judgment.

5. Show the draft in context.
   Be exact about whether the proof is Cranston analysis, Cranston's licensed tax environment, or a live write in the buyer's environment.

6. End with reviewer control.
   Show what the professional reviews, changes, approves, or sends back.

## Do Not Do

- Do not narrate every click.
- Do not describe every screen as an AI feature.
- Do not hide integration limitations.
- Do not end with "any questions?" End with a representative return and a scheduled review step.

## The Demo Must Answer

- What did Cranston receive?
- What work did Cranston do?
- What remains for the professional?
- Where does the result land?
