# Discovery To Proof

Use this on intro calls when the goal is to earn a relevant demo or representative test. Discovery should locate the workflow, not perform a feature tour.

## Call Moves

1. Start with why now.
   Ask why they booked, who feels the pain, and what would make the conversation useful.

2. Locate the workflow.
   Confirm form mix, return volume, tax software, hosting, intake, workpapers, preparers, reviewers, handoffs, bottlenecks, urgency, and decision ownership.

3. Frame the relevant path.
   Connect Cranston to source trail, preparation output, exception handling, and the destination step that maps to their pain.

4. Agree on proof.
   Choose a representative return, confirm authorization and support, define success, include the real operator and reviewer, and put output review on the calendar.

## Questions That Signal Fluency

- What return types create the most reviewer cleanup: 1040s, pass-throughs, or both?
- Do business returns start from clean books, a mapped trial balance, or a package your tax team still has to repair?
- Where does a normal return stall: missing client documents, preparation, review notes, or destination-software entry?
- What does the reviewer check first?
- Which workpaper, source trail, or control point has to remain in the process?
- What would one return need to prove for the operator and reviewer to use this again?

## Test Setup

- Start with a completed, reviewed return so Cranston can be compared with a known answer.
- Use a representative package, not a canned easy example.
- Include a business return or meaningful irregularity when that is the firm's pain.
- Confirm form, year, software edition, hosting, data authorization, and installation ownership before accepting files.





## Other suggested questions
- “I saw your note referencing the previous email—what specifically interested you enough to take the call?” Then get three facts quickly: approximate return volume and mix; current tax software/hosting; and what consumes the most professional time between client documents arriving and the return being review-ready.

- *To confirm identity: gmail with an unknown firm attached to it* - Your first 60 seconds should therefore be qualification, not rapport-building: “Before we jump in, remind me of your firm and your role there—I saw you put down about 400 returns and Drake.” If the identity checks out, ask which Drake environment he uses, the individual/entity mix, who prepares versus reviews, and where the 400-return workflow bogs down.
