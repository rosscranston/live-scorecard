# Cranston Product Mental Model

Use this when explaining Cranston in sales materials, call prep, or follow-up. Keep the promise narrow, traceable, and grounded in the actual workflow.

## Core Positioning

Cranston turns a messy client package into supported, reviewable preparation work. It is not just OCR and it is not a replacement for professional judgment.

Say: "messy package in; supported, reviewable work out."

Avoid: "AI fills forms," "fully automated," "works with everything," or any claim that removes the reviewer from the process.

## The Three-Part Product Model

1. Understand the package.
   Cranston classifies files, uses prior-year context and firm instructions, and identifies missing, duplicate, wrong-year, unreadable, inconsistent, or questionable items.

2. Prepare supported work.
   Cranston maps supported values, assembles workpapers, tracks year-over-year changes, and keeps the source behind each number visible.

3. Hand control to the reviewer.
   The firm reviews the draft, resolves judgment items, requests missing information, approves the return, obtains authorization, signs, and files.

## Boundaries To Keep Clear

- Support depends on form, year, tax engine, edition, hosting environment, and workflow.
- Core federal focus is 1040, 1065, 1120, and 1120-S.
- Current implementation evidence is strongest for Drake Tax Online and CCH Axcess.
- Lacerte and hosted environments are environment-specific.
- Do not infer production support from a logo, integration mention, or marketing page.
- Cranston's SOC 2 examination is in progress. Do not say certified.

## Safe Sentence

"Support depends on the form, year, engine, edition, hosting, and workflow. We will confirm that corridor before the test."
