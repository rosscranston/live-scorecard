# Client-fit rubric

Store `Calculated Fit`, `Ross Fit Override`, and effective `Client Fit` separately.

## Major concerns

A major concern includes:

- unsupported tax software with no acceptable support timeline;
- explicit price sensitivity or economics that do not work;
- no access to a decision maker or an unresolved authority problem.

## Calculation

| Annual return volume | Conditions | Calculated fit |
|---:|---|---|
| More than 1,000 | No major concern | Good |
| 501 to 1,000 | No major concern | Mid |
| 251 to 500 | No major concern | Mid-small |
| Fewer than 500 | Any major concern | Bad |
| 250 or fewer | An explicitly approved lower minimum of 100, 150, or 200 applies and there is no major concern | Mid-small |
| 250 or fewer | Otherwise | Bad |

If volume is unknown, or the evidence is insufficient to test the concerns, use `Needs confirmation`.

## Override

If Ross explicitly gives a fit label after the prospect leaves the call, place it in `Ross Fit Override` and use it as `Client Fit`. Preserve the calculated result and explain the difference in `Fit Rationale`. Do not manufacture an override from Ross's enthusiasm or general commentary.

An explicit `Bad` or `Ignore` assessment also triggers the CRM inclusion gate. Do not create records for that prospect unless Ross grants a record-specific exception. A later correction supersedes an earlier post-call score.
